# Cleaning Product Standards

A clean home shouldn't come with a side of harsh chemicals. That's why we're picky about what goes into our cleaning bags — every product we bring into your home has to meet a few standards first.

## What We Look For

- **EWG-rated for safety.** We reach for products that score well with the Environmental Working Group (EWG), so you can breathe easy knowing there's nothing harsh or hidden in what we're using.
- **Eco-friendly, plant-based formulas.** Our cleaners are biodegradable and made from plant-based or naturally occurring ingredients — kind to your home and to the planet.
- **Vegan and cruelty-free.** Nothing we use is tested on animals or made with animal-derived ingredients.
- **Gentle, but still effective.** Our products cut through dirt, grime, and everyday buildup without harsh chemicals — safe to have around kids, pets, and anyone with sensitivities.

## Why It Matters to Us

We don't think you should have to choose between a clean home and a healthy one. Sticking to these standards means every visit leaves your space fresh and spotless, without leaving behind anything you wouldn't want around your family. It's one more thing you don't have to think about — we've already taken care of it.
