# First Cleaning Savings — No Mandatory Deep Clean Fee

A lot of cleaning companies require a mandatory deep cleaning for a new recurring customer's first visit (to get the home "caught up"), billed at deep-clean pricing, before the membership drops to its normal recurring rate starting visit two. Sky House Cleaning doesn't do this — a new Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly member is billed at their normal recurring rate from visit one, [per the Square Footage and Home Condition terms in each membership's policy](../Policies/Overview.md).

**This is a one-time savings, not a recurring discount.** It's the gap between what a customer's very first cleaning would have cost under a mandatory-deep-clean model and what they actually pay for that first visit. Every visit after that is priced the same either way — the savings only happens once, at signup, because that first deep clean fee never gets charged.

## The short version

Because every recurring rate is a fixed percentage of the Deep Cleaning price (both are derived from the One-Time rate — see [Pricing Matrix](Pricing%20Matrix.md)), the percentage saved on that first visit is exactly the same at every home size:

- **Weekly members save ~53%, once,** on their first cleaning — compared to what a mandatory-deep-clean-first competitor would have charged for it.
- **Bi-Weekly members save ~47%, once.**
- **Monthly members save ~43%, once.**

In dollar terms, that one-time savings ranges from about $100–$120 for the smallest homes up to over $1,000 for the largest ones. It's not a discount that repeats on visit two, visit ten, or any visit after — it's the value of the deep-clean fee they never had to pay in the first place.

## If you share this with a customer

This table is written for your own reference — before sending it to anyone, it's worth trimming it down (most customers only care about their one home size, not the full matrix) and running the framing past the [Verbal Branding](../Branding/Verbal%20Branding.md) guide so it reads warm and plainspoken rather than like a spreadsheet. Whatever wording you land on, keep it clear that this is a one-time savings at signup, not an ongoing discount — customers should never come away thinking they're saving this amount every visit.
