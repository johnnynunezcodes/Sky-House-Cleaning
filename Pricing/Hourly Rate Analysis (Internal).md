# Hourly Rate Analysis — Internal Only

**Not published anywhere on the website.** This is a private reference note for Johnny — it lives in this project folder only, not in the Website codebase, so it's never built or deployed to the live site.

## Why this exists

A gut-check on what Sky House Cleaning effectively charges per hour of job time, across the full pricing matrix (see [Pricing Matrix](Pricing%20Matrix.md)). Useful for thinking about crew pay, staffing decisions, and where the current pricing curve is thinner or thicker per hour of work.

## Method

"Hours" below is the job's total elapsed time — how long the appointment actually runs, start to finish — not a per-person labor count. A 6-hour job is a 6-hour job whether one person does it alone or two people split it and finish together; crew size is a staffing decision that doesn't change the job's duration or price.

Base pace assumption for a Standard cleaning with no maintenance history (One-Time): ~600 sq ft/hr, 1.5-hour minimum. Deep cleaning: ~350 sq ft/hr, 2.5-hour minimum. Move-In/Move-Out: ~380 sq ft/hr, 2.5-hour minimum. No real-world timing data exists yet, so these are reasonable industry-standard assumptions — revisit once you have a feel for actual job lengths.

**Recurring visits get a time discount too.** A home on a regular cleaning schedule doesn't build up as much grime between visits, so each recurring cleaning takes less time than a One-Time cleaning of the same size. The more frequent the visit, the less buildup accumulates — so Weekly is fastest, Bi-Weekly a bit longer, Monthly longer still (a month is enough time for some buildup to return), and One-Time (no maintenance schedule at all) takes the longest. The differences are modest by design, not drastic:

| Frequency | Time vs. One-Time |
|---|---|
| One-Time | baseline (100%) |
| Weekly | ~20% faster |
| Bi-Weekly | ~15% faster |
| Monthly | ~8% faster |

$/hr = price ÷ estimated job hours.

## Notes

- The thinnest hourly rates fall in the 2,500–4,000 sq ft range across every frequency and service type — the pricing curve dips there before recovering at both the small end (minimum-job-charge effect) and the large end (post-August-2026 repricing on the 3,500+ tiers).
- Weekly customers get the deepest hourly discount of the three recurring tiers — that tracks with the -30% weekly price discount being steeper than the ~20% time savings, meaning weekly is a genuinely better deal for the customer even after accounting for the shorter visit. Bi-Weekly and Monthly land closer to One-Time's rate, which also tracks with their shallower price discounts (-20% and -15%).
- These job-length estimates are separate from `JOB_DURATION_MINUTES` in the website's `booking.js`, which uses flat calendar-blocking estimates (3 hrs standard / 4 hrs deep / 4.5 hrs move-in-out) regardless of home size or frequency — fine for blocking calendar time on small-to-mid jobs, but increasingly inaccurate for the largest homes now that pricing tiers go up to 9,999 sq ft, and doesn't yet reflect the recurring-visit time savings described here. Worth revisiting once real job-timing data comes in.
