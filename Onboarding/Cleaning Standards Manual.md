# Cleaning Standards Manual

This is the standard we hold every visit to, no matter which service it is or who's on the job. Clients trust us because the quality is consistent every single time — that consistency is the whole reason Sky House Cleaning exists.

## The Sky House Standard

- **Work top to bottom, back to front.** Dust and debris fall as you clean, so work high surfaces before low ones, and the back of a room before the areas you'll walk back through — that way you're never re-dirtying something you already finished.
- **No shortcuts on the details.** Check corners, baseboards, under furniture you can safely move, and behind items on counters — not just the open, obvious surfaces.
- **Glass and mirrors should be streak-free**, not just wiped. Take the extra minute.
- **Put things back exactly where you found them.** If you move something to clean under or around it, it goes back in its original spot.
- **Leave no trace you were there** — except a clean home. Take your trash out with you unless a client has asked you to empty theirs, and don't leave rags, bottles, or equipment behind.
- **If something's broken, missing, or looks off, say something.** Flag it to the client (or to us, if you're not comfortable raising it directly) before you leave — don't just clean around it and hope it's not noticed.
- **If you're not sure, ask — don't skip it.** A quick question is always better than guessing and getting it wrong.
- **Respect the home.** Shoes off or booties on if asked, no personal calls while you're working, keep personal items to a minimum, and make sure everything is locked up the way you found it when you leave.

## What We Offer

Every service has its own detailed checklist — see [Service Checklists](Service%20Checklists.md) for the full room-by-room breakdown of each one:

- **One-Time Cleaning** — a standard reset clean for the kitchen, bathrooms, living areas, and the whole home.
- **Recurring Cleaning** — the same standard clean, on a weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly schedule.
- **Deep Cleaning** — everything in a standard clean, plus interior cabinets, appliances, walls, baseboards, and interior windows.
- **Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning** — the same expanded scope as Deep Cleaning, for a home in transition.
- **Interior Car Detailing** — a full interior detail: seats, carpets, trunk, dash, console, and door panels.
- **Garage Cleaning & Organization** — decluttering, zone-based organizing, and a full clean of the space.
- **Office & Commercial Cleaning** — recurring janitorial cleaning for offices and small businesses.
- **Post-Construction Cleaning** — a rough clean followed by a final detailed clean for new builds, renovations, and remodels.
- **Airbnb Cleaning** — fast turnover cleaning between guests, so a listing is spotless and guest-ready every time.

Whatever the job, the standard above applies across all of them — the checklists tell you *what* to clean; this page is about *how* we clean it.
