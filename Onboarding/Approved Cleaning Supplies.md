# Approved Cleaning Supplies

We're picky about what goes into a client's home, and that applies no matter whose bottle it comes out of. Whether you're using supplies we provide or bringing your own, every product used on a Sky House Cleaning job needs to meet this standard:

- **EWG-rated for safety** — scores well with the Environmental Working Group, so there's nothing harsh or hidden in what's being used.
- **Eco-friendly, plant-based formulas** — biodegradable, made from plant-based or naturally occurring ingredients.
- **Vegan and cruelty-free** — nothing tested on animals or made with animal-derived ingredients.
- **Gentle, but still effective** — cuts through dirt, grime, and everyday buildup without harsh chemicals, and is safe to have around kids and pets.

If you're ever unsure whether a product meets this bar, check it against the [EWG Guide to Healthy Cleaning](https://www.ewg.org/guides/cleaners) before bringing it into a client's home, or reach out and we'll point you to our approved list.

## What You'll Typically Need

- All-purpose surface cleaner
- Glass & mirror cleaner (streak-free, ammonia-free)
- Bathroom cleaner (for soap scum and hard water buildup)
- Kitchen degreaser
- Wood floor cleaner
- Microfiber cloths — ideally color-coded by area (e.g., one color for bathrooms, another for kitchens, another for general dusting) to avoid cross-contaminating surfaces between rooms
- Vacuum with a HEPA filter
- Mop and microfiber mop pads
- Rubber gloves
- Trash bags

Never bring bleach, ammonia-based cleaners, or anything with a harsh chemical odor into a client's home — if a job seems to call for something stronger than our standard allows, flag it to us rather than reaching for it yourself.
