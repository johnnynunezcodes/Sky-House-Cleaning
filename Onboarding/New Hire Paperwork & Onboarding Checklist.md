# New Hire Paperwork & Onboarding Checklist

Welcome to Sky House Cleaning — we're glad you're joining the team. Here's what to expect as a new employee, and what you'll need to get set up.

## What It Means to Join Us as an Employee

You're joining Sky House Cleaning as a W-2 employee, not as an independent contractor. In practice, that means:

- We withhold taxes from your paycheck and handle the employer side of payroll taxes — you'll receive a W-2 at year-end, not a 1099.
- You're covered by workers' compensation insurance while you're on the job.
- You accrue paid sick leave under California law.
- You're paid at least California and local minimum wage, with overtime pay for anything over 8 hours in a day or 40 hours in a week, and required paid rest breaks and unpaid meal breaks built into your schedule.
- You'll be paid on a regular, set payday — not per job.

## What to Bring on Day One

For your I-9 (federal work authorization form), bring **one** document from List A below, **or one from List B and one from List C**:

- **List A (either one, on its own):** U.S. Passport, or Permanent Resident Card
- **List B (identity):** Driver's license or state ID, or another government-issued photo ID
- **List C (work authorization):** Social Security card, or birth certificate

Documents need to be originals (or certified copies), not photocopies.

## Paperwork You'll Complete

- [ ] **I-9** — Employment Eligibility Verification (we'll walk through this together on day one)
- [ ] **W-4** — federal tax withholding elections
- [ ] **DE 4** — California state tax withholding elections
- [ ] **Direct deposit setup** — enter your bank's routing and account number directly when you complete your onboarding online; no need to bring anything in person for this one. Prefer paper checks instead? Just let us know.
- [ ] **Emergency contact information**
- [ ] **Signed acknowledgment** that you've received and reviewed the [Cleaning Standards Manual](Cleaning%20Standards%20Manual.md), [Service Checklists](Service%20Checklists.md), [Approved Cleaning Supplies](Approved%20Cleaning%20Supplies.md), and [Dress Code](Dress%20Code.md)
- [ ] **Wage Theft Prevention Act notice** — we'll provide this; it spells out your pay rate, payday, and other required details in writing
- [ ] **Paid sick leave notice** — we'll provide this; it covers how your sick leave accrues and how to use it

## Your First Few Shifts

Your first few jobs will be a chance to walk through our standard together before you're working independently — expect some shadowing and hands-on feedback early on rather than being dropped into a job solo on day one. Take your time with the [Cleaning Standards Manual](Cleaning%20Standards%20Manual.md) and the checklist for whichever service types you'll be working — that's exactly what our clients expect every visit, and it's what we'll be checking in on together as you settle in.
