# Service Checklists

A room-by-room checklist for every service we offer. These match what's promised on the website, so completing every item is what "done" looks like on a Sky House Cleaning job.

## One-Time & Recurring Cleaning

### Kitchen
- [ ] Wipe down and spot-clean cabinet faces
- [ ] Clean the stovetop, burners, and drip pans
- [ ] Freshen the inside of the microwave
- [ ] Clean the exterior of appliances
- [ ] Polish the sink and wipe counters and backsplash

### Bathrooms
- [ ] Thoroughly scrub sinks, toilets, tubs, and showers
- [ ] Polish faucets and mirrors
- [ ] Wipe counters and cabinet fronts

### Living Areas & Bedrooms
- [ ] Tidy pillows, blankets, and common areas
- [ ] Make beds (if fresh linens are left out)
- [ ] Dust shelves and blinds
- [ ] Wipe window sills
- [ ] Clean the glass sliding door (if the home has one)

### Throughout the Home
- [ ] Vacuum carpets and sweep/mop hard surfaces
- [ ] Dust interior shelves and closets
- [ ] Wipe down handrails

## Deep Cleaning & Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

Everything above, plus:

### Kitchen
- [ ] Clean the interior of cabinets and drawers
- [ ] Clean the interior of appliances (oven, refrigerator, dishwasher)

### Bathrooms
- [ ] Clean the interior of cabinets and drawers

### Living Areas & Bedrooms
- [ ] Wipe down ceiling fans

### Throughout the Home
- [ ] Wipe down walls and baseboards
- [ ] Clean interior windows
- [ ] Clean the glass sliding door

## Garage Cleaning & Organization

### Organization
- [ ] Sort and declutter items with the client's input (keep, donate, discard)
- [ ] Group items into zones that make sense for them (tools, sports gear, seasonal items, and more)
- [ ] Set up or improve shelving, bins, and storage systems
- [ ] Label bins and storage areas so it's easy to keep up

### Cleaning
- [ ] Sweep and mop the garage floor
- [ ] Wipe down shelves, workbenches, and storage units
- [ ] Clear cobwebs and dust corners, rafters, and light fixtures
- [ ] Wipe down the interior of cabinets and storage bins

## Interior Car Detailing

### Interior Detailing
- [ ] If the vehicle has a lot of personal items inside, bag everything you need to pull out to clean, then put it all back once you're done — as close as you can remember to exactly where it was
- [ ] Full vacuum of seats, carpets, and trunk using a shop vac (skip the truck bed unless the client has specifically asked for it)
- [ ] Wipe down all interior surfaces, including the dash, console, and door panels — for anything that needs heavier-duty cleaning, use the all-purpose cleaner first, then finish with Armor All; if a surface doesn't need the stronger cleaner, go straight to Armor All instead
- [ ] Clean interior and exterior glass and mirrors
- [ ] Spot-treat stains on fabric seats and upholstery — saturate the area with all-purpose cleaner, let it sit for a few minutes, lightly brush it, then vacuum it up
- [ ] Wipe down leather and vinyl surfaces using Armor All car cleaner
- [ ] Clean cup holders, vents, and other tight spots
- [ ] Remove pet hair with the pet hair removal glove, using your hand and fingers on tighter or more detailed spots
- [ ] Clean floor mats — pull them out, saturate with all-purpose cleaner, brush, then rinse or wipe dry with a rag. If a pressure washer is available on-site, use that to spray them off instead. Either way, make sure they're fully dry before putting them back in the vehicle

## Office & Commercial Cleaning

### General Cleaning
- [ ] Empty trash and replace liners
- [ ] Vacuum carpets and mop hard floors
- [ ] Dust desks, surfaces, and furniture
- [ ] Wipe down and disinfect high-touch surfaces (door handles, light switches, handrails)
- [ ] Clean interior glass, mirrors, and partitions

### Restrooms, Break Rooms & Common Areas
- [ ] Clean and disinfect toilets, sinks, and countertops
- [ ] Restock paper towels, toilet paper, and soap when supplies are left on-site
- [ ] Wipe down break room counters, tables, and the exterior of appliances
- [ ] Empty and reline trash bins
- [ ] Wipe down reception desks and shared furniture near entrances

## Post-Construction Cleaning

### Rough Clean
- [ ] Remove construction debris, packaging, and leftover materials
- [ ] Sweep and vacuum up dust and debris from floors
- [ ] Wipe down dust from surfaces, sills, and ledges
- [ ] Clean windows and glass of construction dust, stickers, and labels

### Final Clean
- [ ] Detailed cleaning of kitchens and bathrooms, including inside cabinets and drawers
- [ ] Wipe down all surfaces, baseboards, and trim
- [ ] Clean interior windows, tracks, and sills
- [ ] Vacuum carpets and mop hard floors
- [ ] Wipe down light fixtures and switch plates
- [ ] Remove any remaining dust, stickers, or residue

## Airbnb Cleaning

### Turnover Cleaning
- [ ] Full clean of the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, and living areas between every guest
- [ ] Strip used linens and remake every bed with fresh ones
- [ ] Wash, dry, and restock towels
- [ ] Vacuum and mop all floors
- [ ] Take out trash and reset recycling

### Guest-Ready Touches
- [ ] Restock guest essentials like toilet paper, paper towels, soap, and coffee
- [ ] Fluff pillows, straighten throws, and reset staged decor
- [ ] Wipe down and spot-check appliances
- [ ] Quick walkthrough to flag any damage, missing items, or maintenance issues
- [ ] Turn the space around fast between checkout and check-in
