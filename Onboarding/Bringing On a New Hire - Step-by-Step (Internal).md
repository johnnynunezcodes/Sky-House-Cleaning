# Bringing On a New Hire — Step-by-Step (Internal, Johnny's Eyes Only)

Your own action checklist for the moment an interview goes well and you're ready to bring someone on. Written against your actual current setup: workers' comp through NEXT, payroll and I-9/tax paperwork through Gusto. Not for the employee, hand them the [New Employee Onboarding Packet PDF](Sky%20House%20Cleaning%20-%20New%20Employee%20Onboarding%20Packet.pdf) instead, see step 6 below.

## 1. Extend the Offer and Confirm a Start Date

Verbally or in writing, confirm the role, pay rate, schedule expectations, and start date before anything else moves. Set the rate using [Employee Pay Structure](Employee%20Pay%20Structure.md) (your internal reference, not something they see) — new hires typically start at the bottom of the New/Provisional tier.

## 2. Finalize and Pay for Your Workers' Comp Policy

You have a NEXT quote, but haven't paid to actually bind it yet, and California requires active coverage the moment you have an employee, before their first shift, no grace period. Go back to NEXT, finish the application with real numbers (this hire's pay rate, hours, headcount), and complete the first payment to bind the policy. Save the Certificate of Insurance it generates; you'll need your carrier's name for the Wage Theft Prevention notice in step 5. Don't move on to scheduling a start date until this is actually active, not just quoted.

## 3. Add Them to Gusto

Enter their basic info and set their pay rate. Choose self-onboarding so they fill out their own I-9 Section 1, W-4, DE 4, and direct deposit details directly in Gusto, less work for you, and it keeps their banking info out of your hands entirely.

While you're in there, turn on Gusto's Time Tracking if you haven't already (available on Plus and Premium plans). Employees clock in and out from the Gusto mobile app or a browser, with GPS support to confirm they actually clocked in at the client's address, and their hours sync straight into payroll instead of you tracking them by hand. Worth having active before their first shift rather than adding it later.

## 4. Complete I-9 Section 2 In Person

You have 3 business days from their start date to do this. Review their actual, original documents in person (List A on its own, or List B + List C), then go into Gusto to record what you saw and e-sign your part. See [New Hire Paperwork & Onboarding Checklist](New%20Hire%20Paperwork%20%26%20Onboarding%20Checklist.md) for exactly which documents are acceptable. Since your hires are local, you don't need Gusto's Remote Verification tool or E-Verify, California doesn't require E-Verify for private employers.

## 5. Let Gusto Handle the Compliance Chain

Once you run their first payroll, Gusto handles:

- EDD registration (your state Employer Payroll Tax Account Number), triggered at the right moment automatically
- New-hire reporting (DE 34) within the required 20-day window
- The Wage Theft Prevention Act notice and paid sick leave notice, auto-generated using the pay and carrier info already in your account (this is where your now-active workers' comp carrier name from step 2 gets pulled in)

You don't need to do anything separately for these once the first paycheck is set up.

## 6. Check on the "Know Your Rights" Notice

This is a newer (2026) standalone California notice covering paid sick leave, immigration protections, workers' comp, and union rights. Confirm whether Gusto includes it automatically in their onboarding documents, if not, download it from [calcivilrights.ca.gov](https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/) and hand it over yourself alongside the other required notices.

## 7. Hand Over the Onboarding Packet

Give them the [New Employee Onboarding Packet PDF](Sky%20House%20Cleaning%20-%20New%20Employee%20Onboarding%20Packet.pdf), covers the new-hire checklist, Cleaning Standards Manual, Service Checklists, Approved Cleaning Supplies, and Dress Code. Get their signed acknowledgment that they've received and reviewed it (built into the checklist itself).

## 8. Schedule Their First Few Shifts as Shadow Shifts

Per the onboarding packet: expect to walk the standard together before they're working independently. Don't drop them into a job solo on day one.

## 9. Set Reminders for What Comes Later

- **Workers' comp accuracy** — your NEXT policy is priced off estimated payroll; if a future hire changes your total headcount or payroll meaningfully from what's on file, update it with NEXT so coverage (and premium) still reflects reality.
- **Sick leave accrual** — tracked automatically by Gusto, but worth a periodic glance.
- **Harassment training** — required for everyone once you hit 5 employees (including any contractors/temps counted toward that number). Set a reminder to revisit once you're closer to that headcount.
- **Pay tier revisits** — most employees move from New/Provisional to Experienced within 30–60 days if they're consistently meeting the standard; worth checking back in around then.
