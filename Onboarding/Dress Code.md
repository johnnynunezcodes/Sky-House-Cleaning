# Dress Code

Simple and consistent: **all black.**

- Solid black top and bottom
- Closed-toe, non-slip shoes (you're on your feet on all kinds of floors, so this is as much about safety as it is looks)
- Clean and presentable — you're representing Sky House Cleaning in someone's home or business

If you'd like a Sky House Cleaning logo t-shirt, just ask — we're happy to provide one.
