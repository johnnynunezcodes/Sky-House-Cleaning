Onboarding materials for new Sky House Cleaning employees — what we expect on every job, new-hire paperwork, pay, approved supplies, and dress code.

**Currently hiring as W-2 employees, not contractors.** The contractor-model files below are kept for reference in case that changes later, but aren't part of the current onboarding flow.

A polished PDF version of the employee-facing material lives at [Sky House Cleaning – New Employee Onboarding Packet.pdf](Sky%20House%20Cleaning%20-%20New%20Employee%20Onboarding%20Packet.pdf) — that's the file to actually email a new hire.

## Current (Employee) Materials

- [Setting Up W-2 Employees (California) — Internal](Setting%20Up%20W-2%20Employees%20%28California%29%20-%20Internal.md) — **for Johnny's eyes only.** Everything needed to legally hire and pay employees in California: EDD registration, mandatory workers' comp, payroll setup, required notices, sick leave, overtime/breaks, harassment training. Not included in the employee packet.
- [Cleaner Job Posting](Cleaner%20Job%20Posting.md) — ready-to-post job listing for Indeed, Facebook Jobs, Craigslist, etc. Start here before anything else in this list; everything below assumes you've already got someone.
- [Bringing On a New Hire — Step-by-Step](Bringing%20On%20a%20New%20Hire%20-%20Step-by-Step%20%28Internal%29.md) — **for Johnny's eyes only.** Your own action checklist from "interview went well" through their first shift — offer, Gusto setup, I-9, notices, the onboarding packet, and workers' comp. Not included in the employee packet.
- [New Hire Paperwork & Onboarding Checklist](New%20Hire%20Paperwork%20%26%20Onboarding%20Checklist.md) — what a new employee brings on day one, what they'll sign, and what their first few shifts look like.
- [Employee Pay Structure](Employee%20Pay%20Structure.md) — **for Johnny's eyes only.** Suggested hourly wage tiers, grounded against Oakland/CA minimum wage, with overtime and break rules built in — written as your own planning reference, not for an employee to read. Not included in the employee packet; actual pay rate is discussed with each hire individually.
- [Overtime & Break Rules (California) — Quick Reference](Overtime%20%26%20Break%20Rules%20%28California%29%20-%20Quick%20Reference.md) — **for Johnny's eyes only.** Cheat sheet with worked examples for when 1.5x vs. 2x overtime applies, and what a missed break actually costs. Not included in the employee packet.
- [Cleaning Standards Manual](Cleaning%20Standards%20Manual.md) — the standard we hold every visit to, regardless of service type.
- [Service Checklists](Service%20Checklists.md) — a room-by-room checklist for every service we offer.
- [Interior Car Detailing - Cleaner Checklist.jpg](Interior%20Car%20Detailing%20-%20Cleaner%20Checklist.jpg) — a phone-friendly image version of the Interior Car Detailing checklist, easy to view or text to a cleaner directly.
- [Detallado Interior de Autos - Lista de Verificación.jpg](Detallado%20Interior%20de%20Autos%20-%20Lista%20de%20Verificaci%C3%B3n.jpg) — Spanish (Mexico) translation of the same checklist image, for Spanish-speaking cleaners.
- [Approved Cleaning Supplies](Approved%20Cleaning%20Supplies.md) — the product standard every supply used on a job needs to meet.
- [Dress Code](Dress%20Code.md) — what to wear on a job.
