## About Us

Sky House Cleaning is a residential and commercial cleaning company serving Oakland and the greater Bay Area. We're growing our team and looking for someone detail-oriented, reliable, and easy to work with, someone who takes real pride in leaving a home better than they found it.

## What You'll Do

- Clean homes to a consistent standard: kitchens, bathrooms, living areas, and more, following our checklist for whichever service is booked (standard cleanings, deep cleans, move-in/move-out).
- Work independently once trained, while still communicating clearly with clients and with us.
- Treat every home and everything in it with care and respect.

## What We're Looking For

- Reliable transportation and the ability to travel between job sites in Oakland and the Bay Area.
- Attention to detail, someone who checks the corners, not just the open surfaces.
- A dependable, on-time, professional attitude. Clients are trusting you in their home.
- Prior cleaning experience is a plus, but not required. We'll train you on our standard.
- Comfortable following a dress code (all-black, closed-toe shoes) and simple safety guidelines.

## What You'll Get

- A W-2 position with real employee protections: workers' compensation coverage, paid sick leave, and overtime pay when it applies.
- Hands-on training before you're ever on a job solo.
- Room to grow, pay increases as you build a track record with us.
