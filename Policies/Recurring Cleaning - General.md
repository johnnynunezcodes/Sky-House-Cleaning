# Sky House Cleaning – Subscription Booking Policy

The following booking policies apply to all recurring cleaning memberships, including Weekly, Bi-Weekly, and Monthly services. Each membership frequency has its own minimum commitment term, detailed in that plan's specific policy:

- **Weekly:** 8 completed cleanings (approximately 2 months)
- **Bi-Weekly:** 4 completed cleanings (approximately 2 months)
- **Monthly:** 3 completed cleanings (approximately 3 months)

**1. Skip Policy**
Members may skip up to two scheduled cleanings per rolling 12-month period, provided at least 48 hours' notice is given. Additional skips may be approved at Sky House Cleaning's discretion.
Skipped appointments do not count toward fulfilling a membership's minimum commitment.

**2. Rescheduling**
Appointments may be rescheduled with at least 24 hours' notice, subject to availability.

**3. Late Cancellations**
Appointments canceled 24–48 hours before the scheduled service will incur a 50% cancellation fee.
Appointments canceled less than 24 hours before the scheduled service will incur a 100% cancellation fee.

**4. No-Shows**
If our cleaning professionals arrive for a scheduled appointment and are unable to complete the service because access to the property is unavailable or the appointment is otherwise missed without notice, the appointment will be considered a no-show and charged 100% of the scheduled service price.
No-shows do not count toward fulfilling a membership's minimum commitment.

**5. Booking & Availability**
Appointments are subject to staff availability. Requests to change recurring service days or appointment times will be accommodated whenever possible but cannot be guaranteed.
