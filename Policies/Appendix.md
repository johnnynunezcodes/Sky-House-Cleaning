Booking policies, by service — shown to customers at checkout (a required "I agree" checkbox links to the relevant one before payment). Every policy here should stay consistent in tone and structure unless there's a real reason for a service to differ.

- [One-Time Standard Cleaning](One-Time%20Standard%20Cleaning.md) — payment, rescheduling, cancellation fees, no-shows, square footage confirmation, home condition, satisfaction guarantee, liability, right to refuse service.
- [Deep Cleaning](Deep%20Cleaning.md) — same terms as One-Time Standard Cleaning, plus an expanded home-condition clause for neglected/first-clean homes and a prep note.
- [Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning](Move-In%20Move-Out%20Cleaning.md) — same terms as One-Time Standard Cleaning, plus property access, utilities, and empty-home condition clauses.

- [Recurring Cleaning – General](Recurring%20Cleaning%20-%20General.md) — skip policy, rescheduling, late cancellations, no-shows, and booking/availability terms shared by all three membership frequencies.
- [Recurring Cleaning – Weekly](Recurring%20Cleaning%20-%20Weekly.md) — 8 completed cleanings minimum commitment (~2 months), square footage confirmation, billing, scheduling, cancellation, failed payments.
- [Recurring Cleaning – Bi-Weekly](Recurring%20Cleaning%20-%20Bi-Weekly.md) — 4 completed cleanings minimum commitment (~2 months), square footage confirmation, billing, scheduling, cancellation, failed payments.
- [Recurring Cleaning – Monthly](Recurring%20Cleaning%20-%20Monthly.md) — 3 completed cleanings minimum commitment (~3 months), square footage confirmation, billing, scheduling, cancellation, failed payments.
