# Sky House Cleaning – Monthly Membership Policy

By enrolling in a Monthly Cleaning Membership, you agree to the following terms:

**1. Minimum Commitment**
This membership requires a minimum commitment of 3 completed monthly cleaning services (approximately 3 months).
The minimum commitment is fulfilled by completed recurring cleaning services. Scheduled cleanings that are skipped, canceled, or missed do not count toward fulfilling the minimum commitment and will extend the membership until the required number of completed services has been provided.
If the membership is canceled before the minimum commitment has been fulfilled, the client will be responsible for the remaining payments due under the minimum commitment.

**2. Billing**
Memberships are billed automatically to the payment method on file on the day of each scheduled service, not in advance of it.

**3. Scheduling**
Monthly cleanings are scheduled on the same day and approximate time each month at the time of enrollment. Requests to change your recurring service day are subject to availability.

**4. Membership Cancellation**
After the minimum commitment has been fulfilled, memberships may be canceled at any time by contacting Sky House Cleaning at least 7 days before the next billing date.

**5. Failed Payments**
If a payment cannot be processed, future services may be paused until the outstanding balance has been resolved.
