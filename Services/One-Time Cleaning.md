# One-Time Cleaning

## Service Description

Our Standard Cleaning is a one-time visit to give your home a real reset. Whether you're expecting guests, catching up after a busy stretch, or just want a professional touch, you'll come home to a space that feels clean, cared for, and like itself again.

### Kitchen
We'll leave your kitchen sparkling:
- Wiping down and spot-cleaning cabinet faces
- Cleaning the stovetop, burners, and drip pans
- Freshening the inside of the microwave
- Cleaning the exterior of appliances
- Polishing the sink and wiping counters and backsplash

### Bathrooms
Your bathrooms will look and feel truly clean:
- Thorough scrubbing of sinks, toilets, tubs, and showers
- Polished faucets and sparkling mirrors
- Wiped counters and cabinet fronts

### Living Areas and Bedrooms
We'll help these spaces feel warm and cared for:
- Tidying pillows, blankets, and common areas
- Making beds (if fresh linens are left out)
- Dusting shelves and blinds
- Wiping window sills
- Cleaning the glass sliding door (if your home has one)

### All Interior Areas of the Home
We'll take care of the details throughout your home:
- Vacuuming carpets and sweeping/mopping hard surfaces
- Dusting interior shelves and closets
- Wiping down handrails

This service is a great fit for homes that get cleaned regularly. If it's been a while, or you're dealing with heavier buildup, our Deep Cleaning is the better place to start. Want extras like interior windows, an oven clean, or cabinet interiors? Just let us know at booking and we'll add them on.
