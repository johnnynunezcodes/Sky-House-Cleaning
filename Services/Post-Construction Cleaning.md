# Post-Construction Cleaning

## Service Description

Our Post-Construction Cleaning service clears away the dust and debris that construction, renovation, and remodeling work leaves behind, so a space is ready to use, move into, or hand off. We offer both a rough clean and a final clean, depending on where the project stands.

### Rough Clean
For after the heavy construction work wraps up, but before finish trades move in.
- Removing construction debris, packaging, and leftover materials
- Sweeping and vacuuming up dust and debris from floors
- Wiping down dust from surfaces, sills, and ledges
- Cleaning windows and glass of construction dust, stickers, and labels

### Final Clean
The detailed clean that gets a space truly move-in or open-for-business ready.
- Detailed cleaning of kitchens and bathrooms, including inside cabinets and drawers
- Wiping down all surfaces, baseboards, and trim
- Cleaning interior windows, tracks, and sills
- Vacuuming carpets and mopping hard floors
- Wiping down light fixtures and switch plates
- Removing any remaining dust, stickers, or residue

### Scheduling
For everyone's safety, we schedule post-construction cleaning once the job site is clear of active work. Pricing depends on the space's square footage and how much dust and debris is left behind — we'll review photos or walk the space and put together a straightforward quote.

This one's a great fit for new builds, renovations, and remodels — taking a space from construction-ready to move-in ready.
