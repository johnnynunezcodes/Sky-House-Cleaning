# Car Detailing

## Service Description

Our Interior Car Detailing service brings your vehicle's interior back to life — available as a one-time detail or an ongoing monthly membership. Multiple vehicles can be booked in the same visit.

### Interior Detailing
- Full vacuum of seats, carpets, and trunk
- Wiping down all interior surfaces, including the dash, console, and door panels
- Cleaning interior glass and mirrors
- Spot-treating stains on fabric seats and upholstery
- Wiping down leather and vinyl surfaces
- Cleaning cup holders, vents, and other tight spots

We spot-treat stains on fabric seats and upholstery rather than shampooing and conditioning them, and we wipe down leather or vinyl surfaces rather than conditioning them. Standard vacuuming already picks up everyday pet hair as part of every detail — for heavier shedding, Extensive Pet Hair Removal is available as an add-on (see [Add-Ons](Add-Ons.md)).

### Plans
- **One-Time Interior Detail** — a single visit, no ongoing commitment.
- **Monthly Detailing Membership** — the same service on a recurring monthly visit, at a lower per-visit rate. Requires a 2-month minimum commitment.

### Multiple Vehicles
A customer can book more than one vehicle in the same visit (up to 6). Flat rate per vehicle — no quantity discount.

This one's a great fit for anyone who wants their vehicle's interior looking (and feeling) freshly detailed, whether it's a single refresh or ongoing upkeep.
