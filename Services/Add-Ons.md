# Add-On Services

Add-ons customers can select on top of a service, broken out by which service/frequency they're available for. These match what's live in the pricing configurator on the website's Booking page (for cleaning) and the Interior Car Detailing service page (for detailing).

## Standard Cleaning Add-Ons

Available on: **One-Time, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, and Monthly** Standard Cleaning bookings.

### General Touch-Ups
- Inside Blinds — $20 per set (select quantity)
- Ceiling Fans (Up to 5) — $50 (20 min)
- High Dusting — $40 (40 min)

### Specialty & Laundry Services
- Wash & Dry Laundry — $25 per load (select quantity)
- Fold Laundry — $10 per load (select quantity)
- Inside Washer/Dryer — $20 (15 min)
- Baseboard Deep Clean (Whole Home) — $150 (1 hr 30 min)
- Jetted Tub — $40 (20 min)

### Windows & Interior Detailing
- Interior Windows (Up to 10) — $75 (40 min)
- Interior Windows (11–20) — $140 (1 hr 10 min)
- Window Tracks (Up to 10) — $40 (30 min)

Sliding glass doors are included standard on every service, not offered as a separate add-on.

### Kitchen & Bathroom Upgrades
- Inside Oven — $50 per oven (select quantity, up to 3)
- Inside Fridge — $45 per fridge (select quantity, up to 3)
- Inside Freezer — $35 per freezer (select quantity, up to 3)
- Dishwasher (Inside) — $25 per dishwasher (select quantity, up to 3)
- Cabinet Interior Cleaning — $75 (45 min)

Some homes have more than one oven, fridge, freezer, or dishwasher. Selecting more than one drops the per-unit rate, since there's less setup/context-switching time per additional appliance. The discount is the same across all four (10% off at 2, 15% off at 3):

| Quantity | Inside Oven | Inside Fridge | Inside Freezer | Dishwasher (Inside) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (standard) | $50.00 | $45.00 | $35.00 | $25.00 |
| 2 (10% off) | $45.00 | $40.50 | $31.50 | $22.50 |
| 3 (15% off) | $42.50 | $38.25 | $29.75 | $21.25 |

Rates shown are per unit, at that tier — e.g. 2 ovens at the 2-unit rate is $45.00 × 2 = $90.00 total.

## Deep Cleaning Add-Ons

Available on: **Deep Cleaning** only.

- Laundry (Wash & Dry) — $25 per load (select quantity)

Deep Cleaning already includes Dishwasher (Inside) and Ceiling Fans as part of the base package — see [Deep Cleaning](Deep%20Cleaning.md) — so those aren't offered as add-ons here.

## Multiple Washer/Dryer Discount

Applies to both Wash & Dry Laundry add-ons above (Standard Cleaning and Deep Cleaning). More units means loads can run in parallel, so less hands-on time per load:

- 1 washer/dryer — $25.00 per load (standard rate)
- 2 washer/dryers — $20.00 per load (20% off)
- 3+ washer/dryers — $18.75 per load (25% off)

## Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning Add-Ons

None currently offered. Move-In/Move-Out already includes the deep-detail items (interior cabinets, interior oven/fridge/dishwasher, interior windows, walls & baseboards, ceiling fans) that would otherwise be add-ons on a Standard Cleaning — see [Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning](Move-In%20Move-Out%20Cleaning.md).

## Interior Car Detailing Add-Ons

Available on: **One-Time Interior Detail and Monthly Detailing Membership.**

- Extensive Pet Hair Removal — $35 flat, regardless of how many vehicles are booked in the same visit.

Standard vacuuming already picks up everyday pet hair as part of every detail — this add-on is for heavier shedding that needs extra time.

On the Monthly Detailing Membership, selecting an add-on isn't a one-time charge — the $35 is billed every visit for as long as the membership runs (same recurring-add-on behavior as Standard Cleaning memberships, see above). The website's configurator calls this out to the customer before checkout.

The Monthly Detailing Membership itself requires a 2-month minimum commitment, shown on the plan at checkout.

### Multiple Vehicles

Both plans let a customer book more than one vehicle in the same visit (e.g. two cars), capped at 6 per booking. Flat rate — no quantity discount, unlike the oven/fridge/freezer/dishwasher add-ons above. Each vehicle is billed at the full rate: $275 per vehicle One-Time, $250 per vehicle Monthly (e.g. 2 vehicles One-Time is $275 × 2 = $550 total).
