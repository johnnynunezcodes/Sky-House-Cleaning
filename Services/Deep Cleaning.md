# Deep Cleaning

## Service Description

Our Deep Cleaning goes further than our Standard Cleaning, for homes that need a little extra attention — first-time cleanings, neglected areas, or a seasonal reset. You'll get everything in our Standard Cleaning, plus deeper detailing throughout the home.

### Kitchen
We'll leave your kitchen sparkling, inside and out:
- Wiping down and spot-cleaning cabinet faces
- Cleaning the stovetop, burners, and drip pans
- Freshening the inside of the microwave
- Cleaning the exterior of appliances
- Polishing the sink and wiping counters and backsplash
- Cleaning the interior of cabinets and drawers
- Cleaning the interior of appliances (oven, refrigerator)

### Bathrooms
Your bathrooms will look and feel truly clean:
- Thorough scrubbing of sinks, toilets, tubs, and showers
- Polished faucets and sparkling mirrors
- Wiped counters and cabinet fronts
- Cleaning the interior of cabinets and drawers

### Living Areas and Bedrooms
We'll help these spaces feel warm and cared for:
- Tidying pillows, blankets, and common areas
- Making beds (if fresh linens are left out)
- Dusting shelves and blinds
- Wiping window sills

### All Interior Areas of the Home
We'll take care of the extra details throughout your home:
- Vacuuming carpets and sweeping/mopping hard surfaces
- Dusting interior shelves and closets
- Wiping down handrails
- Wiping down walls and baseboards
- Cleaning interior windows

We recommend this one before starting a Recurring Cleaning membership, or any time your home needs more than a standard refresh.
