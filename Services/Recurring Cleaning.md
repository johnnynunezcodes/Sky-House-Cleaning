# Recurring Cleaning

## Service Description

Our Recurring Cleaning membership brings the same thorough Standard Cleaning to your home on a schedule that works for you — weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly. Regular visits keep your home consistently fresh, catch buildup before it turns into a bigger job, and come at a lower rate than booking one-time.

### Kitchen
Every visit, we'll leave your kitchen sparkling:
- Wiping down and spot-cleaning cabinet faces
- Cleaning the stovetop, burners, and drip pans
- Freshening the inside of the microwave
- Cleaning the exterior of appliances
- Polishing the sink and wiping counters and backsplash

### Bathrooms
Your bathrooms stay looking and feeling clean:
- Thorough scrubbing of sinks, toilets, tubs, and showers
- Polished faucets and sparkling mirrors
- Wiped counters and cabinet fronts

### Living Areas and Bedrooms
We'll keep these spaces feeling warm and cared for:
- Tidying pillows, blankets, and common areas
- Making beds (if fresh linens are left out)
- Dusting shelves and blinds
- Wiping window sills
- Cleaning the glass sliding door (if your home has one)

### All Interior Areas of the Home
We'll take care of the details throughout your home:
- Vacuuming carpets and sweeping/mopping hard surfaces
- Dusting interior shelves and closets
- Wiping down handrails

### Membership Frequencies
- **Weekly** — 30% off One-Time pricing
- **Bi-Weekly** — 20% off One-Time pricing
- **Monthly** — 15% off One-Time pricing

Since your home is on a regular schedule, each visit stays lighter than a one-time reset. If things build up more than expected between visits, an occasional Deep Cleaning add-on can help you catch back up.
