# Professional Garage Cleaning and Organization

## Service Description

Our Professional Garage Cleaning and Organization service turns a cluttered, hard-to-use garage into a space you actually want to walk into. We roll up our sleeves and organize alongside a full clean, so it stays functional long after we leave.

### Organization
- Sorting and decluttering items with your input (keep, donate, discard)
- Grouping items into zones that make sense for you (tools, sports gear, seasonal items, and more)
- Setting up or improving shelving, bins, and storage systems
- Labeling bins and storage areas so it's easy to keep up

### Cleaning
- Sweeping and mopping the garage floor
- Wiping down shelves, workbenches, and storage units
- Clearing cobwebs and dusting corners, rafters, and light fixtures
- Wiping down the interior of cabinets and storage bins

This one's a great fit for a garage that's become a catch-all for clutter, ahead of a move, or as part of a seasonal reset. Need bins or shelving? We can bring those along for an added cost.
