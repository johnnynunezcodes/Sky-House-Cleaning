# Office & Commercial Cleaning

## Service Description

Our Office & Commercial Cleaning service keeps offices, small businesses, and other commercial spaces around the Bay Area consistently clean and welcoming — for the people who work there and the customers who walk through the door. We work around your hours, so cleaning never gets in the way of business.

### General Cleaning
- Emptying trash and replacing liners
- Vacuuming carpets and mopping hard floors
- Dusting desks, surfaces, and furniture
- Wiping down and disinfecting high-touch surfaces, including door handles, light switches, and handrails
- Cleaning interior glass, mirrors, and partitions

### Restrooms & Break Rooms
- Cleaning and disinfecting toilets, sinks, and countertops
- Restocking paper towels, toilet paper, and soap when supplies are left on-site
- Wiping down break room counters, tables, and the exterior of appliances
- Emptying and relining trash bins

### Entryways & Common Areas
- Cleaning entryway glass and doors
- Wiping down reception desks and shared furniture
- Spot-cleaning walls, light switches, and high-traffic areas near entrances

### Frequency
Available weekly, bi-weekly, or on a custom recurring schedule built around your business. Pricing depends on your space's square footage, layout, and how often you'd like us there — we'll walk the space (or work from photos) and put together a straightforward quote.

This one's a great fit for offices, small businesses, and other commercial spaces that want a consistently clean, welcoming environment without adding it to their own to-do list.
